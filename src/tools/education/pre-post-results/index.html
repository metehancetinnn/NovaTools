Pre/Post Results Builder

Last updated: 2026-11-28

For school counselors who give the same short survey before and after a lesson unit or small group. Paste both sets of answers and get matched-student results by objective, a chart, a results table and a summary paragraph for your report. The answers never leave your browser.

1. Paste the answers

Use one row per student and one column per question, with a student code column (not names). Copy the cells from Google Sheets or Excel, or open a CSV export from Google Forms or Microsoft Forms. Both sheets need the same question columns.

2. Match questions to objectives

You will be warned if an objective has no question measuring it.

Target: on a scale question, an answer at or above this value counts as meeting the target (for example 4 or 5 on a 1 to 5 scale). Reverse: for questions where a lower answer is better, such as "I worry before tests".

3.

    What it calculates

    • Matched students: codes are matched between the two sheets, ignoring upper and lower case. Students who answered only once are counted and left out, so the before and after groups are the same people.
    • Share meeting the target for each question: for scale questions, answers at or above your target value; for yes/no questions, "yes"; for knowledge questions, the right answer.
    • Change in percentage points and, for scale questions, the mean before and after, with reverse scoring applied.
    • Objective results: the average of the questions assigned to each objective.

    Writing questions that measure your objectives

    Each objective needs at least one question that a student could answer differently after the lesson. Ask about one thing per question ("I can name two adults at school I can go to for help", not "I feel safe and know who to ask"). Keep the wording, order and answer scale identical in both surveys, and give the post-survey soon after the last session so other events have less time to affect the answers. For knowledge questions, use a single correct option and enter it as the right answer.

    Limits: read before you report

    • The numbers describe this group's answers before and after. There is no comparison group, so they cannot show that the lesson caused the change, and the tool does not run significance tests.
    • In small groups one student moves a percentage a lot; the tool warns you below 10 students.
    • This is not a diagnostic or clinical tool. It does not screen, assess or interpret the wellbeing of any student.
    • It is an independent tool and is not affiliated with or endorsed by ASCA or any school district. Follow your district's rules for student data.

    Privacy

    The answers are processed by JavaScript in this tab. They never leave your browser and are never stored in it; closing the tab discards them. "Save project file" writes a file to your own device only when you click it. Use student codes instead of names: the tool warns you if a column looks like names, email addresses or phone numbers. If you have accepted analytics cookies, the page records only that a result was built or a chart was downloaded, never the answers, codes, questions or title.
